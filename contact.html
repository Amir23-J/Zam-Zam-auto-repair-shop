Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Do I need an appointment?

While walk-ins are welcome, we recommend scheduling an appointment to minimize wait times and ensure we have the right technician available for your specific needs.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), and debit cards. We also work with several financing companies for larger repairs.

Do you offer warranties on repairs?

Yes! We provide warranties on both parts and labor. The specific warranty terms depend on the type of service and parts used. We'll always explain warranty coverage before starting any work.

Can I wait while my car is being serviced?

Absolutely! We have a comfortable waiting area with complimentary Wi-Fi, coffee, and refreshments. For longer repairs, we're happy to recommend nearby restaurants and shops.
