Complete Auto Care Services

From routine maintenance to major repairs, we've got you covered

Oil Change & Maintenance

Starting at $39.99

Regular oil changes are the foundation of vehicle maintenance. Our oil change service keeps your engine running smoothly and extends its lifespan. We use premium oils and filters matched to your vehicle's specifications.

Service Includes:

  • Oil drain and refill (up to 5 quarts of premium oil)
  • Premium oil filter replacement
  • Multi-point vehicle inspection
  • Fluid level check and top-off
  • Tire pressure check and adjustment

Duration: Approximately 30 minutes

Brake Service & Repair

Starting at $199.99

Your brakes are your vehicle's most important safety system. Our comprehensive brake service ensures your vehicle stops safely and reliably every time. We use quality parts and perform thorough inspections.

Service Includes:

  • Brake pad replacement (front or rear)
  • Rotor inspection and resurfacing
  • Brake fluid check and replacement if needed
  • Caliper and hardware inspection
  • Test drive and brake performance verification

Duration: 1-2 hours

Engine Diagnostics

Starting at $89.99

Modern vehicles rely on complex computer systems. Our advanced diagnostic equipment quickly identifies issues, saving you time and money. We provide detailed reports and honest recommendations.

Service Includes:

  • Computer diagnostic scan
  • Error code reading and interpretation
  • Visual engine inspection
  • Detailed diagnostic report
  • Repair recommendations and estimate

Duration: Approximately 1 hour

Tire Services

Starting at $19.99

Proper tire maintenance ensures safety, improves fuel economy, and extends tire life. We offer complete tire services including sales, installation, rotation, balancing, and alignment.

Service Includes:

  • Tire rotation (all four tires)
  • Wheel balance and alignment check
  • Tire pressure adjustment
  • Tread depth inspection
  • Overall tire condition assessment

Duration: 30-60 minutes

AC & Heating Service

  • Blower motor and fan inspection
