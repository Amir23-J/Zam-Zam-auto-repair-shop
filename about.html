Our Story

Built on trust, dedication, and a passion for automotive excellence

Zam Zam Auto Shop was founded in 2010 with a simple mission: to provide Worcester and the surrounding communities with honest, reliable, and affordable automotive services. What started as a small repair shop has grown into a full-service automotive center offering everything from routine maintenance to complex repairs and quality used car sales.

As a family-owned and operated business, we understand the importance of trust and transparency. We treat every customer like family and every vehicle like it's our own. Our team of ASE-certified technicians brings decades of combined experience and a commitment to excellence that sets us apart.

Today, we're proud to be one of Worcester's most trusted auto shops, serving thousands of satisfied customers each year. Whether you need a simple oil change, major engine work, or are looking for your next vehicle, we're here to help with the same dedication and integrity that founded our business.
